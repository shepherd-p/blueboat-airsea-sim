# Stable fixed-wing mission and visible localization evidence

The requested six mission steps remain runway takeoff, waypoint flight,
image-based boat localization, autonomous runway recovery and Sea State 1,
with source backup. This revision addresses four presentation and behavior
problems in a fresh complete closed-loop mission, airsea_edf_stable_01.

The current aircraft is an original MIT-licensed conceptual EDF UAV visual,
1.15 m long with 1.6 m span. It has a lofted fuselage, swept airfoil wings,
canted tails and an enclosed aft duct. There are no exposed propellers.
Its generic native plane FDM is not a calibrated EDF/jet model.

Old raw DataFlash and native-FDM logs showed a sustained pitch limit cycle,
including elevator oscillation; the problem was not solely video cadence.
Pitch FF/P/I/D changed from 0.595723/0.15/0.11/0.007265 to
0.15/0.04/0.10/0.001, pitch time constant from 0.25 to 0.6 s and pitch-rate
limits from 90 to 35 deg/s. Roll demand was also slowed to 60 deg/s and
0.4 s. Wind, turbulence, normal arming and 400 Hz native FDM remain enabled.

On the same two search corridors (mission 3/6, east 260..390 m, altitude
above 10 m), raw pitch-rate absolute P95 fell from 61.075 to 1.554 deg/s.
Raw pitch minus its centered 0.5 s moving mean had RMS 2.113 deg before
and 0.0108 deg after. This diagnostic is calculated identically on both
logs; no attitude smoothing is applied to the flight or rendered aircraft.
It is not a certified frequency-domain test or real-aircraft tuning.

All 25 independent mission checks passed. There were 1,617 original sensor
JPEGs and 12 accepted AprilTag/PnP boat-location estimates (8/4 on the two
passes). Median/P95/max 3D error were 0.665/0.935/0.977 m. The estimator
uses image corners, known 0.432 m tag black-border size, calibrated camera
and ownship MAVLink EKF pose. Boat truth is used only for error evaluation.
Coordinates refer to the tag center fixed to the boat, in ENU from runway
home; the vertical coordinate is not an estimate of boat center of mass.
The first-pass estimates updated waypoint 6 to north 172.841 m through an
acknowledged MAVLink partial mission write.

The uploaded waypoint legs and actual 2D/3D path are exported separately.
Search-corridor cross-track absolute P95 was 6.04 m. Ordinary fly-by
waypoints were approached within 20 m; finite-radius banked turns do not
follow impossible sharp corners exactly. NAV_TAKEOFF completes by heading
and altitude and NAV_LAND defines a glide/flare target, so their XY values
are not fly-through checks. Touchdown was east 10.580 m/north 0.686 m with
0.837 m/s descent, followed by runway stop and automatic disarming.

The 72-second review uses chronological excerpts at original simulation
speed: a newly rendered 1080p30 trajectory replay beside the accumulated
flown path and original archived estimator-input JPEG crops. Each displayed
fix has its original sensor timestamp and age. Stale fixes are explicitly
held observations, not continuous target lock. Future flown trajectory or
future image fixes are not displayed. The main replay cannot create new
flight or vision validation. Original live capture averaged 6.31 fresh
images/s; this is not a claim of 30 fps real-time closed-loop operation.

```bash
bash tools/run_airsea_wsl.sh --out "$PWD/results/new_run" --rtf 0.8
python3 tools/airsea/validate.py --out "$PWD/results/new_run"
python3 tools/airsea/assess_revision.py --run "$PWD/results/new_run" \
  --baseline /path/to/baseline_run
bash tools/replay_airsea_wsl.sh --run "$PWD/results/new_run" \
  --out "$PWD/results/new_replay" --mode review
python3 tools/airsea/check_replay.py --out "$PWD/results/new_replay"
python3 tools/airsea/presentation.py --run "$PWD/results/new_run" \
  --replay "$PWD/results/new_replay" --out "$PWD/results/new_review"
```

Native flat-ground aircraft physics, absent aircraft-water collision
coupling, ideal camera and intermittent two-pass tracking remain limits.
The raw evidence and the presentation replay are kept separately.
